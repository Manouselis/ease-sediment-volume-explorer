How it works

From survey to volume

⌁

Inspect the survey

Start with georeferenced bed elevations in XYZ, GeoTIFF or a PointZ archive. Check the height datum, point coverage and any flagged measurements.

▱

Build the bed surface

Linear TIN interpolates within supported triangles. Experimental Ordinary Kriging predicts at the same calculation locations and estimates prediction uncertainty.

±

Compare and calculate

Compare bed and reference elevations on a shared grid. Sum the differences multiplied by supported area, reporting above and below separately.

What the result means

A design comparison estimates potential dredging material. An earlier survey shows bed change. A single survey track supports a profile comparison along the track. An area volume needs coverage across the canal.

Try it with public data

The demo includes the 3D bed, difference and Kriging uncertainty maps, and downloadable results. To process a new survey, use the local application.
